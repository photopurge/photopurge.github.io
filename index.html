Retire Roadmap

Plan the years ahead, one step at a time

“Will my money last?” Retire Roadmap answers that on a phone, in about five minutes, using a person’s own numbers.

Someone enters the year they would like to retire, what they expect to spend, and what they own. The app projects their finances year by year and shows whether the money lasts through the plan or the year it could run out. They can change any number and see the effect straight away.
